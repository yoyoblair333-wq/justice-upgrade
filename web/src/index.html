An AI arbiter for everyday agreements

Two people write down a plain-English agreement on-chain: a chore split, a borrowed bike, who gets credit for a group project. If they later disagree, one files a dispute, the other responds, and a GenLayer Intelligent Contract gives a bounded ruling: PARTY_A, PARTY_B, SPLIT or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. Every validator re-judges the dispute with its own AI model, and the ruling only stands if they agree. No money moves; the contract records text.

Run a dispute yourself

Try it on testnet: run one full dispute

Your browser signs all five transactions against the live contract and shows each one's real status. It plays both sides with two throwaway testnet accounts, A and B, so you can see the whole lifecycle in one place. In real use, two different people each sign with their own wallet.
